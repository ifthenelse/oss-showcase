# Market discovery and positioning

Use this stage before setting the brand direction, writing the README, or choosing launch channels. In a resumed run, inspect the existing research and handoff first. Reuse evidence when the audience, product, and sources are still current. Refresh gaps or stale findings. Follow the [service preflight](execution.md#service-preflight) before using connectors.

1. Start with the repository, the verified quickstart, and the observed product behavior. Define the likely user, task, audience language, and launch objective.
2. Use the installed **customer-research** skill to find public discussions, reviews, and search intent that speak to the task. Distinguish observed demand signals from hypotheses. Record the query, market, source, date, and limitation. One thread or search result cannot establish market size. Use [Ahrefs or Product Hunt](integrations.md) when scoped access is available. Leave volumes and traffic unknown when unmeasured.
3. Use **competitor-profiling** to compare a direct alternative, an adjacent substitute, and the manual workflow where they exist. Use dated primary sources for features and pricing. Record what each option does well, the job it serves, and what the comparison changes about the showcase. Use **competitors** only for a public-facing comparison that the evidence supports. State when a direct alternative could not be verified.
4. Use **product-marketing** to turn those findings into positioning. Choose an audience, a concrete promise, one demonstrable result, and a small set of search and answer intents. Record each launch claim with its source file, test, or real demo frame. Keep unsupported or unmeasured claims out of the public copy.

Write the dated memo to `.agents/showcase/research.md` and put the resulting positioning in `.agents/product-marketing.md`. If a supporting skill is unavailable, inspect the public sources directly and use the same memo. Use public evidence for market conclusions and verified project evidence for product claims. Pass the memo to [site discovery](publishing.md#discovery) and [launch planning](distribution.md).

## Domain shortlist

Prepare a shortlist only when naming or domain choice is in scope. Rank 3 to 5 candidates by fit, spelling, pronunciation, extension, and verified registration and renewal cost. Check naming conflicts against relevant products, registries and official trademark databases as preliminary screening. Record the exact registrar, date, currency and premium status. Missing DNS or RDAP records do not establish availability. Mark prices and availability unverified when a trusted lookup is unavailable. Obtain separate approval for purchase, DNS or canonical changes.
