# Market research and domain shortlist

Run `research` before asset production when requested or when positioning is unresolved. Run service preflight first. Start from the product facts and code, and record the target audience, market, language, use case and assumptions. Compare direct alternatives, substitutes and the current manual workflow. Check official product documentation, pricing and relevant public discussions. Use Ahrefs and Product Hunt only when their scoped preflight passes. Label demand proxies, sourced observations and untested hypotheses separately. Give each consequential observation a source and date. Research may suggest positioning; source and tests still establish product claims.

Write one concise local memo at `.agents/showcase/research.md` with audience, alternatives, evidence, gaps, recommended positioning and domain shortlist. Start with 3 to 5 credible domains. Rank by pronunciation, spelling, product fit, extension, registration and renewal pricing if verified, and potential naming conflicts. Search relevant products, registries and official trademark databases as preliminary screening. Flag uncertainties without giving legal clearance.

Check exact domains via the chosen registrar's read-only search. Record timestamp, registrar, quoted currency, initial and renewal cost, premium status and source. Use DNS or RDAP as supplementary signals. Missing DNS or RDAP records do not establish availability, price or registrability. Where no authorized registrar lookup works, label the candidate unverified. Domain suggestions are research output. Buying a domain, adding DNS records or switching the canonical requires separately scoped authorization. Request a single decision when choosing the public name or canonical affects downstream work. Continue independent tasks while awaiting it.
